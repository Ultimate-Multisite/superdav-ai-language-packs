# Sponsoring Premium Language-Pack Coverage

Superdav AI Language Packs and all existing community language packs remain free. Sponsorship pays for the compatibility work, translation generation, quality checks, release monitoring, and maintenance needed to support premium plugins and themes that are outside the WordPress.org translation system.

## Published offers

| Offer | Price | Included scope |
|-------|------:|----------------|
| Community campaign | Greater of $299 or $99 per requested language | Feasibility review and initial delivery for one premium product after the campaign threshold is reached |
| Vendor launch | $750 once | Compatibility and rights review, initial extraction, automated structural QA, first delivery, and up to three languages |
| Vendor maintenance | $149/month, 12-month term | One product, up to five languages, release monitoring, qualifying refreshes, priority processing, and quarterly reporting |
| Vendor growth | $349/month, 12-month term | One product family, up to 15 languages, priority refreshes, terminology rules, compatibility fixes, and monthly reporting |
| Vendor portfolio | From $749/month | Multiple products, custom locale coverage, response targets, and reporting |
| Human review | From $0.15 per source word per language; $250 minimum | Separately scoped review by a qualified language professional |

Annual prepayment receives a 15% discount.

Prices are launch offers in US dollars, excluding applicable taxes and payment-processing costs, and may change for new agreements. Existing written agreements retain their stated term and price.

## Request process

1. Open a **Sponsored premium coverage** issue in the project's issue tracker.
2. Provide the public product details, exact text domain, version, requested WordPress locales, and your relationship to the product.
3. Maintainers assess technical feasibility, expected string volume, release frequency, source access, and distribution rights.
4. The maintainers post a written scope decision. A public request is not an accepted sponsorship until this happens.
5. Vendor commercial details and invoices move to a private channel. Never place licence keys, private files, personal data, or payment details in a GitHub issue.
6. Accepted and funded work progresses through the public sponsorship status labels described in `docs/SPONSOR_OPERATIONS.md`.

## Funding and deliverables

[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/superdav42) accepts general project support. General sponsorship does not purchase or reserve a particular language pack.

A contribution is tied to a specific deliverable only when the maintainers explicitly accept the request in writing and identify its funded scope. Vendor agreements are invoiced after feasibility review; the WordPress plugin and GitHub issue tracker never collect card data.

Maintainers do not open a community campaign until technical feasibility and distribution rights are confirmed. The accepted public issue must then identify a campaign reference, approved payment channel, target, attribution method, closing date, and the refund or credit rule that applies if the target is not reached or delivery later becomes impossible.

Only payments recorded against that accepted campaign reference count toward its target. Public progress reports show aggregate funding; contributor identities are published only with consent. GitHub sponsorship received before campaign acceptance remains general project support and is not retrospectively earmarked. Community delivery begins after the accepted campaign reaches its target. The campaign's written terms control refunds or credits; funds are not converted to general support without the contributor's consent.

If feasibility fails before written acceptance, no campaign is opened and no campaign-specific payment should be requested. If an accepted vendor engagement later becomes impossible, its written agreement controls refund, credit, or replacement options.

## What sponsorship includes

- Compatibility and translatable-string assessment.
- AI-assisted translation generation for the accepted locales.
- Placeholder, format, and package-structure validation.
- Standard WordPress language-pack delivery through this project.
- Maintenance for qualifying upstream releases during an active maintenance term.
- Coverage and delivery reporting at the cadence stated by the tier.

## Boundaries

- Sponsorship does not make AI output equivalent to certified human translation.
- Legal, medical, financial, safety-critical, and regulated text requires separately scoped professional review.
- A vendor must have the authority to permit the agreed extraction and distribution workflow.
- Sponsors do not receive editorial control over unrelated project decisions.
- Existing language packs remain available when sponsorship ends, but updates and compatibility guarantees stop at the end of the paid term.
- Official WordPress.org translations continue to take precedence whenever available.

## Privacy

Sponsorship requests are public GitHub issues. Do not submit private contact details or product files. GitHub processes account and sponsorship data under its own terms. Vendor billing and private materials are handled outside the plugin after scope acceptance.

The WordPress plugin does not transmit sponsorship data automatically. It opens the project issue tracker or GitHub Sponsors only when an administrator clicks the relevant link, without appending site details.

## Cancellation and renewal

- Vendor maintenance terms run for 12 months unless the written agreement says otherwise.
- Sponsors may decline renewal before the next term.
- Coverage enters `sponsor:paused` when an invoice is overdue, required product access is unavailable, distribution rights change, or the supported product becomes technically incompatible.
- Maintainers provide the reason and the corrective action needed before resuming coverage.

## Reporting

Reports use privacy-safe aggregate data where available:

- accepted products and locales;
- current supported version;
- packages generated and refreshed;
- translation and structural-QA counts;
- unresolved compatibility or review items;
- delivery time from qualifying release to refreshed package.
